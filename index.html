Meet Yaya

Hi, I'm Yaya.

A few years ago, I took a leap of faith and moved from South Africa to the United States as an au pair. Like many people, I had so many questions and had to figure out the process one step at a time. Through research, personal experience, and learning from others who successfully made the move, I gained valuable knowledge that helped me navigate my journey.

Today, I'm proud to be continuing my journey as a student in the United States. Looking back, I know how overwhelming the process can feel, especially when you don't know where to start. That's exactly why I created these guides.

I'm from Queenstown in the Eastern Cape and also lived in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha). I understand the unique questions and challenges South Africans face when planning a move to the USA.

Every big journey begins with a single step of courage.
